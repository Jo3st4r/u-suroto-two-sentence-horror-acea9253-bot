## The story

> I taught my daughter to count the stairs before bed so she would know if anyone was hiding under them. Last night she counted twelve, and our staircase has eleven.

2 sentences, 1 ritual, 0 monsters named: the first sentence is a protective ritual — a parent teaching a child to count the stairs so she will know if anyone is hiding under them — and the second sentence makes it worse on a second read: last night she counted twelve, and the staircase has eleven. The ritual worked, and that is the horror. Nothing is explained and no monster is named; the reader fills in what the twelfth step is.
